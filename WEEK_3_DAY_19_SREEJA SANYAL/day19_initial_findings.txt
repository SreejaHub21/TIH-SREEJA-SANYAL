Day 19 performed controlled hyperparameter experiments using the frozen Day 13 training and validation datasets.
The test set was not loaded or used for model selection.
Random Forest and XGBoost were evaluated using controlled changes to selected hyperparameters.
Validation MAE was used as the primary selection metric, with Validation RMSE as the secondary criterion and Validation R2 as an additional performance measure.
Linear Regression from Day 15 and MLP from Day 17 were included in the overall model comparison as reference candidate models.
For Remaining Charging Time, the best validated configuration was Random Forest (RF_1_Baseline_Tuned) with Validation MAE=66.0090, Validation RMSE=85.3417, and Validation R2=0.9965.
For SOC, the best validated configuration was XGBoost (XGB_2_More_Regularization) with Validation MAE=5.7672, Validation RMSE=8.3488, and Validation R2=0.8926.
